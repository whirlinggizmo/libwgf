# Asteroids' assets

Made for the game, under libwgf's MIT license:

- `sounds/*.ogg`: generated by `tools/gen_sounds.py`, Ogg Vorbis made by ffmpeg from its synthesis; `sounds.json` holds the samples' SHA-256, which `--check` holds the files to.
- `scenes/asteroids.scene`: the game's prefabs, written by hand.
