Generated by `tools/measure_sizes.py --write` from `benchmarks.json`, the baseline CI holds libwgf to (`--check`: a program more than 1% and 1 KB larger, gzipped, fails). Every program is a release web build, as a page ships it; sizes in KB. Why each number is what it is, and how it was found, is HISTORY.md's ("Web sizes, measured" and after).

Measured from libwgf a01e588 2026-10-12, libwgt 8871e04 2026-10-05, wgrender-c 25ef6fd 2026-10-05, with Emscripten 5.0.7.

### Why rows differ

- <a id="differs-app-touch"></a>**app-touch**: libwgf has no pointer picking: the coin is hit-tested by hand, where libwgt links its pointer and picking ([its header](../examples/c/app-touch/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-model"></a>**gfx-model**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: a toy car of its own (tools/gen_model.py) in place of the animated character ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-model/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-materials"></a>**gfx-materials**: libwgf has no skinning yet: a gold torus in place of the animated character, and a generated sphere in place of the file's ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-materials/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-lights"></a>**gfx-lights**: libwgf has no skinning or lit sprites yet: generated capsules in place of the animated characters, and no lit billboards ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-lights/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-shadows"></a>**gfx-shadows**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: the toy car in place of the walking character, and its readout fixed labels with no formatting ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-shadows/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-instancing"></a>**gfx-instancing**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: six of the toy car in place of the walking characters, and its readout fixed labels with no formatting ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-instancing/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-environment"></a>**gfx-environment**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: the toy car in place of the walking character, and its readout fixed labels with no formatting ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-environment/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-render-target"></a>**gfx-render-target**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: the toy car drives the circle in place of the walking character ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-render-target/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-postprocess"></a>**gfx-postprocess**: libwgf has no skinning or animation yet: the toy car in place of the walking character ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-postprocess/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-text3d"></a>**gfx-text3d**: libwgf has no picking yet: the hover highlight and the cube made pickable or not are left out ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-text3d/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-sprite2d"></a>**gfx-sprite2d**: libwgf has no 3D and no alpha picking: the animated model behind the sprites, its lights, and picking are left out ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-sprite2d/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-gfx-tilemap"></a>**gfx-tilemap**: libwgf has no picking: a 2D stage and a 2D camera (written before its 3D sprites), coins hit-tested by hand ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-tilemap/main.c) lists every difference).
- <a id="differs-asset-loading"></a>**asset-loading**: libwgf has no 3D: textures and sounds of like weight in place of wgrender-c's models and environments ([its header](../examples/c/asset-loading/main.c) lists every difference).
