### Notes

- <a id="note-gfx-model"></a>**gfx-model**: libwgf's is its own: the toy car (tools/gen_model.py), its parts found by name, under a camera that circles or flies; the references' is an animated character (libwgf's gfx-skinning) ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-model/main.c)).
- <a id="note-gfx-materials"></a>**gfx-materials**: libwgf's spheres are generated and a gold torus stands where wgrender-c's animated character is ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-materials/main.c)).
- <a id="note-gfx-lights"></a>**gfx-lights**: libwgf's models are generated capsules where wgrender-c's are animated characters, and its lit billboards have no normal map ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-lights/main.c)).
- <a id="note-gfx-shaders"></a>**gfx-shaders**: libwgf's leaves out the logo's sprite effects (libwgt's sprite effects shader isn't ported, and a 2D stage's sprite doesn't use a material); the rest is libwgt's ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-shaders/main.c)).
- <a id="note-gfx-tilemap"></a>**gfx-tilemap**: libwgf's is its own: a Tiled map, its objects made actors from a scene's prefabs, a body sliding on its cells, and a painted trail; the references' is a map of sprites with coins the pointer picks ([its header](../examples/c/gfx-tilemap/main.c)).
- <a id="note-asset-loading"></a>**asset-loading**: libwgf's loads textures and sounds of like weight where wgrender-c's loads models and environments ([its header](../examples/c/asset-loading/main.c)).
